Three little breakthroughs.

  1. Crack the code.

    Each letter has a number. Use the given clues to work out the pattern. Enter a few numbers and check them. Once A and B are solved, “Extend A → B” fills the rest.

  2. Search the grid.

    The words get their numbers from your key. Drag across a matching sequence, or tap its first and last cells. Words run in straight lines, in any of eight directions, and can share cells.

  3. Solve the secret.

    The unused cells become letter tiles. Put them in order to discover one final word.

A wrong check, word selection, or final answer counts as one mistake. Hints are counted separately. Tapping, typing, and clearing a selection do not count. There is no timer.

Keyboard: Tab to a cell and press Enter or Space to choose an endpoint. Arrow keys move around the board; Escape cancels a selection.
